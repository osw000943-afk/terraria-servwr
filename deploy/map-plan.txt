Mobile Survival starter map
===========================

Target: Medium world, normal difficulty, vanilla mobile clients.

Spawn and safe hub
- Build a compact spawn platform near the initial spawn point.
- Place guide, nurse, merchant, and storage buildings around the hub.
- Use non-corruptible blocks and walls for the hub shell.
- Keep a second, clearly marked emergency shelter below the hub.

Travel and activity zones
- East road: surface exploration and early-game resource route.
- West road: fishing, housing expansion, and later NPC housing.
- Underground entrance: mine access with signs and return platforms.
- Arena: a separate open area for events and boss fights, away from homes.
- Farms: place mob, herb, and fishing farms outside the hub and arena.

Mobile-safe protection
- Use walls, doors, signs, locked chests, and a server password.
- Keep valuable storage inside the hub and back up the world before major builds.
- Do not advertise a building as server-protected: vanilla mobile has no
  server-side region protection or permission plugin.

Implementation
- Generate or build the world with the official mobile-compatible client.
- Copy the resulting .wld file to /var/lib/terraria/worlds/ as the terraria user.
- Set world= in serverconfig.txt and verify the exact client/server versions.
